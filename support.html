How to play

  • Move with the stick on the left. Hold the attack button to attack; your abilities sit beside it.
  • Tap an enemy to lock your attacks on it; tap the ground to go back to automatic targeting.
  • Earn XP near fallen enemies. When you level up, tap + to improve an ability.
  • Towers and the Root take much less damage from a guardian who arrives without creeps, so push together with your wave.
  • Pause, then Leave to menu, to continue a match against the AI later.

Playing with a friend

One player chooses Play with a friend and creates a match; the other enters the join code. Both phones need the same version of Grove Duel and an internet connection.

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/Sezos/groveduel-site/issues. Please include your phone model and the game version from the store listing.
