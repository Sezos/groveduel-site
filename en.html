Grove Duel

Four guardians. One lane. Five-minute duels.

A free MOBA made for your phone, built in Mongolia. Play in English or Mongolian.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

iPhone, iPad, Android · Online matches on our server in Ulaanbaatar

A whole match in five minutes

Push with your creeps, level up, spend your gold and bring down the enemy's heart. Your first match teaches you as you go.

  1. MoveWalk with the stick at bottom left. Step aside and their shots miss you.
  2. AimTap a skill to aim at the nearest threat, or drag to aim by hand. Here Snapjaw lines up Vine Hook.
  3. WinKeep your Grove Heart alive and bring down the Blight Root.
  4. ShopBuy items at your base, sell them back, and upgrade them into stronger versions.

Four guardians

Each guardian has three skills and a completely different way to play. Pick one and watch it move.

Seedling

Fights from range

Keeps enemies at a distance and heals up when health runs low.

  • Seed ShotFires a seed that hits the first enemy in its path.
  • BloomHeals you and hurts the enemies around you.
  • DashA quick dash in a direction. Use it to escape.

Bramble

Fights up close

Walks into the middle of the fight and takes the hits.

  • Thorn LungeLunges forward and hits the first enemy in the way.
  • Bark GuardHardens your bark: you take less damage for a moment.
  • Bramble QuakeSlams the ground and hurts every enemy around you.

Sporecap

Controls the fight

Slows enemies, roots them in place and hops out of reach.

  • Spore BurstThrows spores that hurt and slow the enemies they touch.
  • TangleA shot that hurts the enemy it hits and roots them for a moment.
  • Puff StepA short hop away that slows the enemies left behind.

Snapjaw

Catches

Drags enemies in with a vine and holds them with a bite.

  • Vine HookThrows a vine that catches the first enemy and drags them to you.
  • SnapBites the enemy in reach and holds it in place for a moment.
  • GulpSwallows an enemy creep whole and heals you.

Alone or with friends

Play the AI offline, or other players online. Everything except Ranked works without an account.

Offline

  • Learn to playYour first duel walks you through moving, attacking, skills and the shop.
  • Quick duelAgainst the AI on Easy, Normal or Hard.
  • TrialsFive duels against the Blight. Beat one to open the next.
  • Pick it up laterLeave a match against the AI and carry on later, even after closing the game.

Online

  • With a friendCreate a match and share its code.
  • Quick matchMeet another player. If nobody is online, a clearly labelled practice match against the AI starts instead.
  • 2v2 roomsTwo players a side on six team maps, with bots in the empty seats.
  • RankedSign in with Game Center or Google Play Games, play ranked 1v1 and 2v2, and climb from Seedling to Ancient Grove.

A phone call pauses an online match for up to a minute, and a dropped connection reconnects on its own.

For streamers and clubs

A match takes five to eight minutes, so it fits between segments of a stream.

  • Invite a viewer with a code and play them 1v1.
  • Fill a 2v2 room with friends; bots take any empty seats.
  • The server is in Ulaanbaatar: 20–40 ms from anywhere in Mongolia.
